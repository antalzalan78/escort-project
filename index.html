Privacy & cookies

Last updated: July 2026

Your privacy matters. This page explains, in plain language, what happens with your data when you visit this website or contact me.

Who is responsible

This website is run by Milan, an independent companion based in Tilburg, the Netherlands. For any privacy question you can reach me through the contact details on this site.

What data I collect

Only what you choose to send me. Through the contact form that means your name, contact detail, preferred date, duration, location and message. If you contact me by WhatsApp, Telegram or email, I receive whatever you share there.

Why and how long

I use this information only to answer your message and arrange a meeting. I keep it no longer than needed for that, and delete it afterwards. I never sell or share your details with third parties for marketing.

The contact form

The contact form is processed by Web3Forms, a service that forwards your message to my inbox. Your submission passes through their servers and may be stored for up to 30 days on the free plan. See web3forms.com for their own privacy terms.

Cookies & local storage

This site uses no tracking, advertising or analytics cookies. It stores two small items in your own browser purely so the site works: your 18+ confirmation and your chosen language. These stay on your device, are never sent anywhere, and are not used to track you.

Fonts & external content

The fonts are hosted on this website itself, so no data is sent to Google or other font providers. Apart from the privacy-friendly visitor counter described below, the site loads no third-party trackers.

Visitor statistics

To see how many people visit, this site keeps a simple, anonymous counter in my own private database. It sets no cookies, records no IP addresses and stores no personal data. It only increases a total visit number, nothing that could identify you.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time what data of yours I hold, and request that I correct or delete it. Just send a message and I will take care of it.
